## Rights and release gate

The ONNX card declares Apache-2.0. The Kev card declares Apache-2.0 for the adapter and head and for its Qwen base; the pinned Qwen base has an Apache-2.0 license file. The ONNX and Kev source repositories do not include standalone license files, and the Kev card says training datasets have their own licenses. These declarations are useful evidence, but the missing conversion input record and outstanding notice and dataset-rights review mean redistribution of the exact converted artifact has not been approved.

The evidence record remains `pending`. It requires the ONNX publisher's source revision and export/quantization recipe, exact tool versions and configuration, and a completed rights review for the exact shipped files before release. A chronological candidate or an ONNX graph's self-reported producer version cannot satisfy those requirements. `pnpm run check:model-provenance` therefore fails closed.
